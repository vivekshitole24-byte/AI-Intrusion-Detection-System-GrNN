Why Granulation Changes Everything

Standard neural networks have a fundamental flaw — they inspect network traffic at a single, fixed scale.

❌ Standard AI / ML Model
  • Inspects each packet in isolation — no temporal memory
  • Misses slow brute-force attacks spread over hours
  • Drowns minority attacks in majority-class noise
  • Cannot explain which feature level triggered the alert
  • Runs heavy inference on every single packet — computationally expensive
✅ Granular Neural Network (GrNN)
  • Processes traffic at micro, meso, and macro scale simultaneously
  • Macro granule catches APT beaconing over hours of traffic history
  • Class-weighted attention isolates rare attack patterns from noise
  • Reports exactly which granule flagged the threat and with what weight
  • Micro-subnet acts as fast early-exit gate — 70% compute reduction
